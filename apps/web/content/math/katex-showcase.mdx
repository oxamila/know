Know renders LaTeX at **build time** with [KaTeX](https://katex.org) — the formulas below are
plain HTML in the server response, so they work without JavaScript and are crawlable by search
engines.

## Inline math

Wrap expressions in single dollars: $E = mc^2$, the quadratic formula
$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$, or Euler's identity $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

Fractions, roots and scripts all work inline: $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$,
$\sqrt[3]{27}$, $a_{i_j}^{n}$.

## Display math

Double dollars produce centered display equations:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} \, dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(a+b)^2 &= a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \\
(a-b)^2 &= a^2 - 2ab + b^2
\end{aligned}
$$

## Fenced code blocks

````md
```math
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
```
````

Which renders as:

```math
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
```

## Matrices and systems

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
x \\
y
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
e \\
f
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Greek letters and operators

$\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \Delta, \Sigma$, and operators such as
$\nabla \times \mathbf{F}$, $\lim_{n \to \infty}$, $\prod_{i=1}^{n}$,
$\binom{n}{k}$.
